This month's focus :
When the data you need is the data you shouldn't collect

Imagine you are using an AI system to analyse health data in a conflict setting. You want to know whether certain populations are systematically underrepresented.

You might need information about ethnicity, origin, or affiliation. But collecting or storing these attributes can itself create a security risk. In a conflict zone, sensitive data is not simply a privacy concern: if it leaks, it can put patients or healthcare facilities at risk.

This creates a difficult paradox: how can we detect unfairness without collecting information that could endanger the people we are trying to protect?

PARSE addresses this by asking not only what is in the data, but what is missing and why. People may be absent because they could not reach a health facility, were displaced, or were never included in the reporting system.
